## THEME:

Paper trading is an online system where users receive virtual money and can buy and sell shares of real companies based on actual market data. The system tracks the value of the user's portfolio and their profit or loss, and allows them to compete with other users.

## 10 FEATURES:
 1. Registration
 2. Stock search
 3. Buying and selling
 4. User portfolio statistics
 5. Watchlist
 6. Leaderboard
 7. Company news
 8. Price alerts
 9. Compare stocks
10. Trading history

## Running:
1. Create a local .env ([get yours here](https://finnhub.io/login))
   *Example: `FINNHUB_API_KEY=your_api_key_here`*
2. `docker compose up --build`
